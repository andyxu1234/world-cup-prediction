## 赔率模块：修复 Bookmaker.odds 关系映射启动报错
- 远程服务启动报 `Could not determine join condition between parent/child tables on relationship Bookmaker.odds`。根因：`MatchOdd.bookmaker_id` 在模型层只声明为 `Integer`，未加 `ForeignKey("bookmakers.id")`，而 `Bookmaker.odds`(odds.py:50) 与 `MatchOdd.bookmaker`(odds.py:98) 互相 `back_populates`，SQLAlchemy 推导不出连接条件 → 启动 mapper 配置即报错（cache_warmer 触发）。
